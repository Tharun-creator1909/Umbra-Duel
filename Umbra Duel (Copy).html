No motions needed: Fireball, Uppercut, Shadow Step (Ronin) and the Ultimate each have their own key. The classic motions still work too: Fireball is down, forward, Special; Uppercut is forward, down, down-forward, Special; Shadow Step is down + Special. Hold back to block; ultimates can't be blocked. Esc pauses, R restarts the match.

Combos are forgiving: inputs are buffered for about 10 frames, and a move that connects (or is blocked) can be cancelled into the next one. Light into Light or Heavy; Light or Heavy into any Special or the Ultimate; any Special into the Ultimate. Press Auto combo repeatedly (Y for P1) to run Light, Light, Heavy, special, Ultimate on its own; the Ultimate joins in when your meter is full. Each combo hit scales damage down 8% (to a 40% floor) and hitstun is long, so strings land reliably. Meter fills as you hit and get hit; the Ultimate costs a full bar.
